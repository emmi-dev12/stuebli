# Stübli design

Native macOS editor; operate mode. Design direction chosen under the user's instruction to proceed: an architectural worktable with a large room canvas, quiet native controls, warm neutral surfaces, and a restrained green selection accent. System typography and SF Symbols keep the installation small and controls familiar.

The scene is the primary content. A left inspector exposes dimensions and object selection. A segmented control switches the canvas between plan and 3D. Status and validation remain visible beneath the canvas. Sample dimensions and generic furniture are clearly labeled; no IKEA accuracy or photorealism claims.

Use native focus, keyboard, window resizing, dialogs, and accessibility labels. Keep geometry in metres and show editable numeric fields with units. Empty, invalid, and unsaved states must have an actionable explanation.
